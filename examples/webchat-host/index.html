Need help with an order?

This page embeds the OCSO web chat with one tag:

<script src="…/ocso-webchat.js" data-key="…" async></script>

The launcher appears bottom-right once the chat is ready. Conversations continue across reloads.

Signed-in customers

After your own login, sign a short-lived HS256 JWT (sub = your customer id) with the channel's host identity secret on your server and pass it to OcsoWebChat.identify(token).
