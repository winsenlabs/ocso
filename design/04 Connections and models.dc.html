How adding a server works

03AuthenticateOAuth 2.1 in a popup; OCSO keeps only a secret reference.
04ReviewEvery tool, its side-effect class and required scopes.
05ApproveChoose scope, agents allowed and confirmation policy.
06ActiveSchemas sync, health starts, agents see the tools.
